Inbox blocks

Your Gmail home page, split into People, Codes, Accounts, Google and Other.

Connection

  1. In your Apps Script project: Deploy → New deployment → Web app, with Execute as: Me and Who has access: Only myself.
  2. Copy the Web app URL (it ends in /exec) and paste it here.

Clean my inbox automatically

Automated mail leaves the inbox: job alerts, newsletters and Google news right away, codes after 30 minutes, bills and orders after 3 days. Nothing is deleted. Everything stays in its label, and starred mail always stays.

Automated mail already in your inbox will be moved out now.

Always animate

Pip and the blocks move gently: new mail glows, Pip hops, blinks and naps. Off means following your computer's “reduce motion” setting.

Your computer currently asks apps to reduce motion, so animations stay off unless you turn this on.

On your phone

Open the same link on your phone while signed in to this Google account. You get the blocks as a page, and codes copy with one tap. Then use Add to Home Screen to keep it like an app.
